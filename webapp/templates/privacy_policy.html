Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZigZag Signals handles user data in the Telegram bot and Web App available at zigzag-bot-package.fly.dev.

Data We Collect

We may process and store the following data:

  • Telegram user ID, language preference and timezone.
  • Favorites/watchlist selected by the user.
  • Subscription status, trial status and subscription expiration date.
  • Payment invoice identifiers and payment status received through Crypto Pay webhooks.
  • Basic technical logs required to keep the service stable and secure.

How We Use Data

We use this data to:

  • Provide access to the bot, Web App and trading signal features.
  • Save user preferences such as language, timezone and favorites.
  • Manage free trials, paid subscriptions and payment confirmations.
  • Detect errors, prevent abuse and improve service reliability.

Payments

Payments are processed through Crypto Pay. We do not store private wallet keys, bank card data or full payment credentials. We only store the information needed to confirm that a payment was completed and to activate the subscription.

Third-Party Services

The service may use Telegram, Crypto Pay, hosting providers and market data providers. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We keep user data only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain subscription records, prevent duplicate trial usage and comply with operational requirements.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the bot owner through Telegram. Some payment or security records may be retained when necessary for fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal compliance.

Trading Risk Notice

ZigZag Signals provides analytical information only. It is not financial advice, investment advice or a guarantee of profit. Trading financial instruments involves risk, and users are responsible for their own decisions.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the bot owner through the Telegram bot profile.
